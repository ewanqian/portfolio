# TIMER / Volumetric Desert
## 体积化沙粒与空间时间结构 · 2026—

**Project status: In development.** A proposed spatial edition of the existing *TIMER* artwork, not a released Vision Pro application.

### 中文 / 对外短文

《TIMER》的新版本将时间的推进、停滞与逆转转译为由大量沙粒构成的体积化空间。以 Apple Vision Pro 的全景观看为首个体验方向，作品研究粒子、尺度与观看位置之间的关系，并为未来的环幕、穹幕及大型多屏空间保留可重新编排的影像结构。

### English / Public short copy

*Volumetric Desert* extends *TIMER* into a spatial field of densely simulated sand. Time is experienced through accumulation, suspension and reversal rather than represented by a clock face. The first study is conceived for panoramic viewing in Apple Vision Pro, with an adaptable image structure for future panoramic, dome and large-format multi-screen environments.

### Formal directions

- particulate mass and spatial depth rather than flat particle decoration;
- accumulation, exposure, suspension and reversal as compositional behaviours;
- camera / viewer position and apparent scale as part of the work;
- adjustable output grammar for panoramic headsets, curved screens and dome projection.
