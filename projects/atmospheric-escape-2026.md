# ATMOSPHERIC ESCAPE

实时视觉系统 · 2026

轨道、航天器结构与粒子形态，在现场屏幕中展开。

ATMOSPHERIC ESCAPE 是一件面向现场演出的实时视觉作品。图像以轨道线、坐标、结构剖面与粒子形态构成视觉语言，将观看带入一个不断变化的空间系统：有时像观察天体的仪器，有时像拆解中的航天器，有时又成为延展至屏幕边缘的粒子场。

作品包含 CELESTIAL、SIGNAL、SPACECRAFT、ENERGY 四个 sequence。在 2026 年 9 月 5 日上海 ENTROPY 演出中，图像同时进入正面环绕屏幕与顶部屏幕。观众的视线在两个平面之间移动，表演者的身体与操作台则为巨大的图像提供尺度。

这里呈现的是作品在 ENTROPY 现场的一个版本：轨道图形、结构变化、粒子运动，以及它们与现场空间的关系。

## English

Realtime visual system · 2026

Orbital lines, spacecraft structures and particle forms unfold across a live performance space.

ATMOSPHERIC ESCAPE is a realtime visual work for live performance. Its visual language brings together orbital paths, coordinates, structural diagrams and particle forms. The image shifts between the appearance of an observational instrument, a spacecraft being taken apart and a particle field extending towards the edges of the screen.

The work comprises four sequences: CELESTIAL, SIGNAL, SPACECRAFT and ENERGY. At ENTROPY in Shanghai on 5 September 2026, the imagery occupied both a front-facing wraparound screen and an overhead screen. Viewers moved their attention between the two surfaces, while the performers and their equipment gave the image a physical scale.

These selected moments show the work in its ENTROPY setting: orbital graphics, changing structures, particle motion and their relationship to the performance space.
