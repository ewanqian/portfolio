# HeroDome — Immersive Opening Study
## 超大画幅演出开场与可重编排音乐结构

**Status: early production study / not a delivered show.**

HeroDome 研究如何把现有作品中可被演奏的音乐时间、流动结构与空间图像，重新组织为面向大型沉浸场馆的开场影像。它不要求为每块屏幕重新发明一件作品，而是先确定视线、中心、屏幕拓扑和演出分段，再把 Drop Flow / TIMER 中适合的视觉与音乐材料转译到新的空间。

### Short presentation text

以已建立的声音与图像结构为基础，重新编排环幕、穹幕与超大画幅场馆的开场段落。HeroDome 从空间尺度、观众视线和演出时间出发，探索可跨屏幕、实时系统和空间观看媒介迁移的沉浸式视听语言。

### Scope

- one 30–90 s sequence sketch;
- one venue proxy / stage plot (not a certified venue reproduction);
- an audio and visual cue score;
- one Arena-compatible playback or realtime test;
- fallback output, single-screen version and image credit log.

Do not imply a Sphere Studios collaboration or an actual Sphere booking. This is a study. Keep it in Research / Archive until the first complete screen-mapping test exists.

Related: Drop Flow, TIMER, Scene Forge and Performable Music.
