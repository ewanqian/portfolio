# Drop Flow / Capturing Movement
## 动作捕捉与空间演出研究 · 2026—

**Project status: In development.** This is a new chapter of the existing Drop Flow series, not a separate completed premiere.

### 中文 / 对外短文

以既有舞者动作捕捉数据为起点，重新编排身体运动、视觉生成与剧场空间。《Drop Flow》的下一阶段将把身体留下的运动轨迹转化为可重演、可进入的实时影像，并探索舞台、增强现实与数字场景之间的观看关系。

### English / Public short copy

Building on motion-capture material from earlier performances, the next chapter of *Drop Flow* reworks movement into a realtime image language for choreography and spatial staging. The research follows how a body's recorded gestures can be replayed, transformed and experienced across theatre, augmented reality and digital environments.
