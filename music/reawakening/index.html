《Reawakening》捕捉休眠中的躯体开始恢复活动的时刻。低频脉冲、碎片化节奏与回响中的合成器纹理，在机械与有机之间，勾勒复苏最初的变化。

Reawakening explores the first movements of a dormant body. Low-frequency pulses, fragmented rhythms and reverberant synthesizer textures trace a gradual return to motion, between the mechanical and the organic.

关于艺术家

钱誉文 Ewan Qian，媒介艺术家。创作横跨声音、动态影像与实时系统，以音画表演和电子声音探索时间、节奏与空间感知。

Ewan Qian is a media artist working across sound, moving image and realtime systems. Through audiovisual performance and electronic sound, he explores time, rhythm and spatial perception.
