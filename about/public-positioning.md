# Ewan Qian / 钱誉文

## 中文简介

钱誉文 / Ewan Qian 是一位媒体艺术家与现场视听表演者。他的实践关注声音、动态影像、时间与空间如何在实时过程中共同塑造人的感知。

他的创作横跨现场视听表演、沉浸式装置与生成系统。数字媒介在其中不仅用于生产图像，也被视为一种响应性的环境：作品随着声音、时间、互动与人的在场逐渐展开。

他的作品经常运用声音分析、空间扫描、点云影像与实时视觉系统，将信号、运动和物理空间转化为不断变化的视觉结构。近期的工作进一步关注艺术家、系统与观众之间的关系，以及如何创造能够回应现场、却不完全可预测或受直接控制的体验。

## English Bio

Ewan Qian is a media artist and live audiovisual performer. His practice explores how sound, moving image, time, and space shape perception in real time.

Working across live audiovisual performance, immersive installation, and generative systems, he approaches digital media not simply as a means of producing images, but as a responsive environment that unfolds through sound, duration, interaction, and presence.

His work often draws on sound analysis, spatial scanning, point-cloud imagery, and real-time visual systems to translate signals, movement, and physical spaces into shifting visual structures. Recent projects further examine the relationship between artist, system, and audience, particularly through experiences that remain responsive without becoming entirely predictable or directly controllable.
