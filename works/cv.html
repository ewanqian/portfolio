钱誉文 Ewan Qian

以声音、动态影像、时间与空间为材料，创作现场视听、生成影像与空间影像，并研究实时系统如何进入表演和公共空间。

Ewan Qian is a media artist and live audiovisual performer based in Shanghai, working across generative imagery, spatial media, interactive systems and performance.
