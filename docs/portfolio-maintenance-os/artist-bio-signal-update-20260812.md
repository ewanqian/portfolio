## AI practice chronology

Future public material should not describe AI as a direction that only entered the practice in 2026.

Current confirmed chronology:

- 2025: AI-assisted interactive web, text and simple game prototypes were already part of the practice. 《新媒体艺术家模拟器》 is one relevant node.
- 2025.07: the Hangzhou Center Art Museum workshop “游戏化 AI 生活解构师” explicitly used AI Agent and gamified methods to guide participants in building personal simulators.
- 2026 early year / around April: AI-assisted coding, MCP / agent tools had entered realtime performance preparation, engineering workflow and creative-tool development.
- 2026 current: AI is also used for project retrieval, version organization, prototype development and the longer-term personal creative archive.

Public framing should therefore treat AI as an established and expanding working strand spanning web interaction, text, game prototypes, workshops, coding assistance, realtime systems and personal creative infrastructure.
