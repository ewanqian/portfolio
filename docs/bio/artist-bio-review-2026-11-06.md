## 中文 · 标准版

钱誉文（Ewan Qian，1998 年生）是媒体艺术家、现场视听表演者，VIRTURA 艺术创作组织发起人与核心创作者之一。他以声音、动态影像、时间与空间为创作材料，探索影像进入现场、身体与建筑之后，观看与感知如何被重新组织。其工作涵盖现场视听、空间影像、生成艺术、空间采集与实时视觉系统，并延伸至人工智能辅助的交互与创作方法。

他持续发展《控时者 / TIMER》与《滴流 / Drop Flow》等合作作品系列。《控时者》曾于 2024 年杭州国际电子音乐节演出，并在 UFO Terminal「加载…权限 2」呈现；《滴流》则从音画现场发展至 2025 年首届中国（杭州）艺术与科技国际双年展开幕式及其他空间版本。其个人影像作品《以太碎片 / Ether Fragment》曾于第十届上海西岸艺术与设计博览会 DREAM·video 单元呈现。

他参与的现场合作包括 2025 年深圳 BO LIVE《机械光合：TITAN 的全息声林》、舟山 CAN Festival，以及 2026 年 REACTOR GRAND OPENING DAY1 和 ENTROPY 等演出。他同时开展创意编程与视听创作教育，包括 2025 年杭州中心美术馆《游戏化 AI 生活解构师》及 2026 年《极简输入：构建视听系统》《舞台复刻》公开工作坊。

他的实践持续追问：当技术扩展创作的可能性，艺术家的判断、经验与形式选择如何继续塑造作品。

## English · Standard

Ewan Qian (b. 1998) is a media artist and live audiovisual performer, and an initiator and core creative member of VIRTURA. Working with sound, moving images, time and space, he investigates how images reshape perception when they enter performances, bodies and architectural settings. His practice spans live audiovisual performance, spatial imaging, generative art, spatial capture and real-time visual systems, extending into AI-assisted interaction and creative methodologies.

His ongoing collaborative series include TIMER and Drop Flow. TIMER was performed at the Hangzhou International Electronic Music Festival in 2024 and presented at UFO Terminal's Loading… Access 2; Drop Flow developed from audiovisual performance into spatial versions, including the opening programme of the 2025 China (Hangzhou) Art and Technology International Biennale. His video work Ether Fragment was presented in the DREAM·video programme of the 10th West Bund Art & Design Fair in Shanghai.

His live collaborations include Mechanical Photosynthesis: TITAN's Holographic Sound Forest at BO LIVE, Shenzhen (2025), CAN Festival, Zhoushan (2025), and REACTOR GRAND OPENING DAY1 and ENTROPY in Shanghai (2026). He has also led and co-led workshops including Gamified AI Life Deconstructors (Hangzhou Center Art Museum, 2025), Minimal Input — Building an Audiovisual System (2026), and Stage Replica (2026).

His work asks how artistic judgment, accumulated experience and formal choices continue to shape artistic practice as technology expands the possibilities of production.
