# Ewan Qian — Selected Artist CV / 2026-11-06
*Application-oriented working edition; distinct from the comprehensive Master CV.*

**EWAN QIAN / 钱誉文**  
MEDIA ARTIST · LIVE AUDIOVISUAL PERFORMER · SHANGHAI, CHINA  
b. 1998 · VIRTURA, initiator and core creative member  
Portfolio: https://ewanqian.github.io/portfolio

Ewan Qian is a media artist and live audiovisual performer working with sound, moving images, time and space. His practice encompasses audiovisual performances, spatial image-making, generative systems and scanning-based digital environments. In his collaborative artwork series TIMER and Drop Flow, visual systems develop through successive performances, installations and spatial adaptations. He also creates AI-assisted creative tools and public workshops on audiovisual interaction and stage previsualisation.
