# No Further Input Required / 无需进一步输入

独立视觉作品 · 2026

矩形彼此嵌套，线条在黑白之间形成密度与纵深。

《无需进一步输入》围绕矩形、边界与重复展开。大小不一的框形在同一图像中叠合：局部像层层打开的窗口，整体又形成向内收束的空间。细线的疏密变化使平面产生深度，观看在单个结构与整体秩序之间往返。

2026 年 9 月，作品在外滩大会首届 AI 概念艺术展「纠缠，而后日以作夜」中展出。展陈将图像置于独立的屏幕框架中；同一作品的图像也用于展览海报，与展览标题、参展者信息和时间地点共同构成另一种观看方式。

屏幕中的作品与海报中的图像共享结构语言，各自承担不同的呈现方式。

## English

Independent visual work · 2026

Nested rectangles turn lines into density and depth.

No Further Input Required works with rectangles, boundaries and repetition. Frames of different sizes overlap within a single image. Individual areas resemble windows opening into further layers, while the composition as a whole draws the eye inward. Changes in line density give depth to the flat surface, moving attention between individual structures and the larger arrangement.

In September 2026, the work was exhibited in Entangled: Day for Night, the AI Concept Art Exhibition at Inclusion Conference on the Bund. The exhibition placed the image within a standalone screen enclosure. Imagery from the work also appeared on the exhibition poster, where it entered a different relationship with the title, artist list and event information.

The screen presentation and the poster share a structural language while offering distinct ways of encountering the image.
