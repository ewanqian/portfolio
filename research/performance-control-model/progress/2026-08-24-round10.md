## RESTORED LONG VISUAL IDENTITY

Structured activation again creates a multi-second visual system.

Step events now perturb that system internally rather than defining the entire visual identity.

```text
MATERIAL ACTIVATION
→ 3–5 s visual behaviour

SEQUENCE STEP
→ internal impulse / perturbation
```

FX capsules also receive their own multi-second visual envelopes.

## PERFORMANCE CONTRACT

Perform mode now exposes sixteen direct material choices but retains few actions:

```text
structured tap/key → TRIGGER
Shift + structured key → LATCH
FX tap/key → one-shot CAPSULE
EVOLVE → advance latched structures
RELEASE → remove persistent layers
```

Safety bounds:

```text
MAX LATCH = 6
MAX AUDIO VOICES = 56
shared compressor / master
role-aware random
collision-limited mutation
```
