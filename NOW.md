## Publication rule

A changing project can live here before it appears on the website homepage.

Promotion path:

```text
active research
→ documented
→ public-safe
→ case-study-ready
→ selected work
```

A project does **not** become a Selected Work just because it is new, technically ambitious, or currently important to development.
