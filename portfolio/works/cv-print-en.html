EWAN QIAN钱誉文

MEDIA ARTIST · LIVE AUDIOVISUAL · SPATIAL IMAGE
Shanghai, China / b. 1998 / VIRTURA founding artist
ewanqian.site/works/cv.html

Ewan Qian is a Shanghai-based media artist, live audiovisual performer and spatial image maker, and a founding artist of VIRTURA. His practice spans generative moving image, spatial capture, XR, interactive systems and real-time performance.

Ongoing collaborations include TIMER and Drop Flow. Drop Flow was selected for the China · Hangzhou Art and Technology Biennale; his independent moving-image work Ether Fragment was presented at West Bund Art & Design. His work also encompasses live visual performance and public workshops.
